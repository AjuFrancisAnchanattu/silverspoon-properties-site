Founder

Leena Parwani

Leena is the Founder of Silver Spoon Properties LLC, a private client representation practice built exclusively for buyers in Dubai's premium property market. Her firm specializes in off-plan and secondary market acquisitions, developer negotiations, and building long-term property portfolios for discerning clients.

With a focus on discretion and buyer-first advocacy, she works with end-users, investors, and overseas buyers who want a single point of accountability throughout the purchase — from first shortlisting through negotiation and handover.

Leena has built partnerships with ten of Dubai's leading developers, giving clients access to off-market and pre-launch inventory before it reaches the public market. Every recommendation is weighed against a client's existing portfolio and long-term goals, not evaluated as a single transaction.

Since founding Silver Spoon Properties, she has represented buyers exclusively — never developers or sellers — a distinction that shapes every negotiation, every shortlist, and every relationship she builds.
