About Silver Spoon Properties LLC

Silver Spoon Properties LLC is not a sales machine, and it was never built to move generic inventory. It exists for one purpose: to find and secure a life with a view worth owning, and the privacy to enjoy it.

Golf-front, waterfront, and beachfront villas: addresses where the view itself is the asset. This is the only kind of property Silver Spoon looks for, nothing sold because it's easy to sell, only what's genuinely extraordinary.

To hire Silver Spoon is to hire Leena Parwani directly, to have her personally source, evaluate, and secure the most beautiful view properties in Dubai on your behalf. That means primary access as golf-view developments launch, and private, off-market opportunities on the secondary side that never reach a public listing.

This is representation for people who know exactly what a view is worth, and who want someone equally particular on their side.

I've called the UAE home for 28 years, long enough to have watched this market move through several cycles, not just one boom. That's the role I take on: your eyes and advocate on the ground, working on your time zone and your terms.

Vision and Values

Leena's purpose in real estate extends beyond closing a sale. Her vision is rooted in representing buyers with the same discipline a family office would expect: discretion, independent judgment, and long-term thinking, in a market built to move fast.

At the center of her approach is a simple belief: a client's trust matters more than any single transaction. That means being willing to say no to a project everyone else is pushing, and yes only to what genuinely fits a client's goals.

These values shape every relationship she builds and every recommendation she makes.

How representation works

Off-market access, first

Most listings reach the market only after every broker in Dubai has already shown them. Developer relationships mean private clients often see units before they're publicly available, sometimes before a project has even launched.

Negotiation on your side

A developer's sales team negotiates for the developer. Leena negotiates purely for the buyer on price, payment structure, and terms, bringing a private client's interests to a table usually stacked against them.

Allocation, not a waitlist

In-demand launches sell out through allocation, not chronological sign-up. Established developer partnerships mean serious buyers get a real shot at the units they actually want, not whatever's left over.

Built for the portfolio, not the sale

Every purchase is weighed against what a client already owns and where the market is heading, not evaluated in isolation. The goal is a stronger portfolio three purchases from now, not one closed deal today.
