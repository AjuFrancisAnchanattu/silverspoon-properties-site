Private Client Representation

One advocate, working exclusively for you — from the first discovery call through handover and beyond.

The Difference

Whose side is your agent really on?

In Dubai, almost every agent has access to the same developers, the same units, and the same price sheets. What differs — and what actually protects you — is who they're working for.

Typical in the market

A Regular Agent

  • Often juggling a developer's sales targets alongside your search
  • Shows whatever's easiest to close, not necessarily what fits you
  • Same public listings every other buyer already sees
  • Negotiating room limited by what the developer allows
  • Relationship typically ends at signature
Silver Spoon Properties

Your Private Client Representative

  • Works exclusively for you, not for a developer's sales target
  • Sources off-market and pre-launch inventory before public release
  • Negotiates price, payment plan and terms purely for your outcome
  • Every recommendation weighed against your wider portfolio
  • Present through EOI, booking, and handover — and beyond
How It Works

A clear process, start to finish

Six steps, always in the same order — so you always know what's happening next and why.

01

Discovery Call

A focused conversation on goals, budget, timeline, and must-haves — the brief every recommendation is measured against.

02

Shortlisting

A curated shortlist across developments and, where relevant, off-market and pre-launch options — not a scroll through public listings.

03

Private Viewings

Accompanied viewings with direct, unfiltered feedback on unit, building, and location — including what to avoid.

04

Negotiation

Price, payment plan, and terms negotiated purely for your outcome, using developer relationships built over years.

05

EOI & Booking

Expression of interest, booking form, and deposit handled correctly and on time, with every document checked before signature.

06

Handover

Present at handover to verify snagging and documentation, with support that continues well after keys are collected.

Who This Is For

Representation built around your goals

The process stays the same — what changes is what each recommendation is weighed against.

End-Users

Buying a home to live in, not just an asset. Guidance weighted toward lifestyle fit, location, and long-term livability.

Investors

Focused on yield, capital appreciation, and exit timing. Recommendations benchmarked against comparable stock and market data.

Overseas Buyers

Purchasing remotely, often without ever setting foot in Dubai. Full representation from first call to handover, wherever you are.

Portfolio Buyers

Building a multi-property position across developments. Every new purchase weighed against what you already own.
